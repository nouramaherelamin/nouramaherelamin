## About Me

I'm a Computer & Information student at the **Egyptian Chinese University** with a broad interest in technology and a hands-on approach to learning.

My experience so far has taken me across software development, web technologies, data analysis, machine learning, databases, and embedded systems. I enjoy exploring different areas, working on practical projects, and learning through experimentation and problem solving.

Rather than limiting myself to one technology or career path, I focus on building a strong technical foundation and developing the ability to learn, adapt, and apply new tools to real problems.

This profile reflects my current journey and is designed to grow alongside it.
